Long vowels: AA = आ, II = ई/ी, UU = ऊ/ू. AH at a Sanskrit word ending represents visarga ः.
Retroflex: tongue curls back. Devanagari is the clearest distinction on this site.

Dental vs. retroflex on this site: dental uses TA / THA / DA / DHA / NA; retroflex uses TTA / TTHA / DDA / DDHA / NNA. Therefore कटी = KATTII: the TT marks retroflex ट and II marks long ī.
Three “s” sounds: SHA = श, SHHA = ष, SA = स.
